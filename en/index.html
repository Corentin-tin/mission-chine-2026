    Fairs & visits

    CIIF 2026 · Shanghai

    12–13 OctNECC Hongqiao

    China's largest industrial technology fair: over 3,000 exhibitors across 300,000 m², 186,000 trade visitors in 2025. Focus on the two themed shows RS (Robotics Show) and AIMS (Next-Gen Intelligent Manufacturing Show).

    Visitor badge: pre-register on ciif-expo.com, unless the organiser handles it for the group (TBC).

    Agibot 智元机器人

    13 Oct morningoptionalLingang

    Shanghai humanoid-robot start-up founded in 2023 by Peng Zhihui (ex-Huawei). Its factory in the Lingang special zone builds bipedal and wheeled humanoids, in series production since late 2024 / 2025: Shanghai's first humanoid production line.

    Lingang is about 1 h from Jing'an. The visit must end before the 13:45 meeting at the hotel. Useful questions: production volumes, unit price, data-collection model.

    Macao

    13 evening → 14 Oct
    • Casinos: 21+ only, no photos on the gaming floor, carry your passport.
    • Free casino shuttles from the ferry terminals and the airport.
    • Currency: pataca (MOP). Hong Kong dollars are accepted at par, about 3% against you.

    Canton Fair · 140th session

    15–16 OctPhase 1: 15–19 OctPazhou

    Phase 1 categories: electronics and electrical goods, household appliances, lighting, machinery and production equipment, hardware and tools, vehicles and mobility, new energy. Industrial robotics and automation are on show.

    • Personal badge, shown with your passport at every entry. Lost badge: replaced at the visitor centre in ~1 h (passport required).
    • 3 zones, 16 halls, 1.5 million m²: ~20 min on foot to cross. Entrance A = the only reliable meeting point.
    • Comfortable shoes and a bottle of water. The fair Wi-Fi is overloaded: rely on your eSIM.
    • Photograph every interesting stand together with its business card. Have samples delivered to the hotel.

    Overseas buyer badge: apply through the Buyer Service System, collect it with your original passport, unless the organiser handles it (TBC).

    Borders & customs

    TerritoryBelgian passportStay
    Mainland ChinaVisa-free (scheme extended to 31/12/2026)30 d / entry
    MacaoVisa-free90 d

    Other nationalities in the group: check your own entry rules.

    • Three border crossings: leaving mainland China for Macao, re-entering Macao → Guangzhou, then leaving from Guangzhou. Each entry starts a new 30-day period.
    • Arrival card: the first one (flight EK 304) is filled in ahead of time. For Macao → Guangzhou, plan a new card with the Ritz-Carlton address.
    • Cash: up to USD 5,000 (≈ €4,500) in foreign currency without declaring it to Chinese customs.
    • Fingerprints are taken on entry into China.
    • EES (the new Schengen Entry/Exit System): does not apply to EU citizens. Non-EU travellers in the group must register in the Travel to Europe app before departure.

    On the ground

    Internet access

    A travel eSIM (China + Macao) routes traffic through a foreign network: WhatsApp, Google, Gmail and LinkedIn work on mobile data without a VPN. On hotel and fair Wi-Fi in China, these services are blocked.

    On landing: turn on the eSIM for data and its roaming, turn off roaming on the Belgian SIM but keep it active for SMS (Alipay and bank codes). Dubai is not covered: use the airport Wi-Fi.

    VPN (backup)

    Useful for the laptop on hotel Wi-Fi. Install and test it before departure: it cannot be downloaded from inside China. Ask IT about the company VPN.

    Stealth mode on, HK / Japan / Singapore servers, VPN off for Alipay, WeChat and Didi.

    Payments

    Mainland China pays for almost everything without cash. Alipay as your main option, WeChat Pay as a backup. European cards rarely work directly. Keep a little CNY in cash. In Macao: Alipay, MOP and HKD.

    Power plugs

    China: types A/C/I, 220 V. Thin European plugs often fit, Belgian earthed plugs don't. Macao: type G (British). Bring a universal adapter with USB ports.

    Weather

    26–32 °C

    Light clothes + a layer for the air conditioning (fairs, hotels, plane). End of typhoon season in Macao/Guangzhou: low but real risk.

    Useful apps

    Alipay · WeChat · Amap or Baidu Maps (Google Maps is poor in China) · Didi · offline translator · expotrip · NIA 12367

    Business etiquette

    Plenty of business cards, handed over with both hands, Chinese side facing the other person. Contacts are swapped by WeChat QR code rather than email. Many conversations happen in Mandarin: the translator will come in handy.
